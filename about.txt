--- [ 4. EXTENDED ABOUT / SUMMARY ] ---
(For use in release notes, social media posts, or portfolio documentation)

Rubby Room Chat is a modern communication platform designed for instant, barrier-free messaging. Users can instantly join or create custom chat rooms simply by entering a room name and username. By sharing a generated link, friends can hop into the room seamlessly. 

Beyond human-to-human interaction, Rubby Room Chat integrates Google's cutting-edge Generative AI (Gemini) to provide an intelligent on-demand conversational assistant. Built with React 18, Firebase, Express, and styled with a signature neon cyberpunk aesthetic, Rubby Room Chat delivers a fast, responsive, and visually stunning web experience.

Developed by Siddhesh More and Yuvraj Chaudhari under RubbySoft.
